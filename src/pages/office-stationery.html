The problem

The fourth time you explain the same specification.

  • Diwali comes round and somebody has to find last year’s order, work out what stock the diaries were, and describe it to a vendor who was not involved.
  • The new batch of pens is a slightly different weight and the logo sits lower.
  • Nobody wrote down the lanyard length, so this year’s are shorter, and 200 people are wearing them.
  • The order goes in late because it took two weeks to reconstruct what the order even was.

You are not disorganised. You are doing a supplier’s record-keeping for them.

Repeat orders fail on memory, not on printing. When the specification lives in an email thread from fourteen months ago, every reorder is a fresh negotiation with a fresh chance to drift.
