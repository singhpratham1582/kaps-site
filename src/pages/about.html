The story

It started as one person who would not sign off on a bad sheet.

KAPS was founded in New Delhi in 1995 as Kothari Advertising and Printing Services. It began the way most print businesses begin, with one person who knew presses and took the work personally.

What kept it going for three decades was not growth. It was the boring thing: organisations that could not afford a mistake found that they did not have to check. So they came back the following year, and the year after that.

Today KAPS is being built into something more structured than a print shop. Same presses, same standard, but with design, packaging, occasion print and merchandise brought under one operation rather than farmed out to whoever is free.

The reason is simple enough. A client who trusts you with an annual report will ask whether you can also do the packaging, the event collateral and the gifting. For most of the last three decades the answer was to find someone. Now the answer is in-house.

How we work

Three things we do not do.

Saying what you refuse is more persuasive than saying what you promise.

We do not print a file we know is wrong

If the artwork will fail on press or the stock is wrong for the job, we say so before it is printed. Sometimes that costs us the order. It costs less than the reprint.

We do not treat a short run as a favour

Fifty pieces or fifty thousand, the specification is held either way. A small job is not a nuisance and it is not priced like one.

We do not let the record live in somebody’s head

Stock, ink values, finish, dimensions and folds are written down once. Every run after that is measured against the record rather than against memory.

Finishing

The last operation is the one nobody sees.

Folding, binding, trimming and packing happen under the same roof as the press. Nothing leaves to be finished somewhere else and come back wrong.
