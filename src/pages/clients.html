What they have in common

One thing.

A court, a hospital, an airline and a steel company do not have much in common as businesses. What they share is that none of them can send out a print run and hope.

A court’s material has to be correct. An airline’s has to be identical across every cabin. A hospital’s has to be legible under pressure by someone who is not reading carefully.

That is the kind of work KAPS has been doing for thirty-one years, and it is the reason the list looks the way it does.
