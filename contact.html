What happens next

Exactly what happens next.

Publishing the agenda is the point. Nobody sends a file if they think it starts a sales process.

You send the file

You send the file, or describe the job

Whatever state it is in. AI, PDF, CDR, PSD. If it is in something else, send it anyway.

We open it and check it

Bleed, resolution, colour mode, embedded fonts, die line, and whether the stock you have in mind suits the job.

We tell you what we found

In plain language. What will fail, what will look wrong, and what to change.

If you want a quote, we send one

With a delivery date on it. If you do not want one, you will not get one.

That is the end of it, unless you continue

You keep the check either way.

Even if we are not the right printer

The promise holds either way.

Some of the jobs that come to us are better off elsewhere. A standard business card at a standard size on standard stock will be cheaper at a portal, and we will tell you so rather than quote you.

You will still get the file check, and you will still leave knowing what to fix. That is the promise, and it holds whether or not there is an order in it for us.

The first conversation is usually as much about subtraction as anything else. If you have asked for 500 brochures and 200 leaflets would do the job, we will say so. If the finish you have chosen will not survive the way the piece is going to be handled, we will say so. Nobody in print makes money on that paragraph. It is still the right first conversation to have.
