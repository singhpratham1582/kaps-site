Same file

Most print buyers do not have one printer. They have four: one for stationery, one who does packaging, someone’s cousin for the event banners, and a portal for whatever is urgent.

That is four specifications, four quality standards, four people to chase, and four opportunities for the brand colour to drift a shade.

Seven disciplines under one operation means the specification is written once and applied to all of it. The blue on the annual report is the blue on the carton and the blue on the banner, because it is the same record and the same press floor.

Print technologies

The methods, and what each one is for.

Offset

The standard for volume. Best cost per unit above a few hundred pieces, and the most consistent colour across a long run.

Digital offset

For short runs and fast turnarounds, where setting up an offset job would cost more than it saves.

Letterpress

Impression printing. The type is pressed into the paper, so you feel it as much as see it.

UV printing

High-gloss coating, applied to the whole sheet or to selected areas.

Foil printing

Metallic foil applied under heat and pressure.

Sticker and label printing

Roll or sheet, cut to shape.
