The problem

Nobody tells you what you actually need.

  • You ask a printer for a quote and they ask what GSM you want. You do not know what GSM is, and now you feel like you should.
  • The minimum order is 1,000 and you need 100, so you either overspend or you go to a portal and hope.
  • You made the logo yourself and it looks correct on screen. Nobody has told you that it is 72 DPI and will print like a photograph of a logo.
  • You are not sure whether you need brochures at all, and the person quoting you has no reason to say no.

None of this means you are doing it wrong. It means the industry assumes you already know, and prices accordingly.

What you actually need

Honestly, in order.

Telling a new founder what to skip is the cheapest useful thing a printer can do, and almost nobody does it.

Order first

Visiting cards. Still the thing you will hand to somebody in the first month.

Order second

Letterheads and envelopes, but only if you send physical correspondence. Many businesses now do not, and the pack sits in a drawer.

Order when you have a reason

Brochures. They date fast and they are usually printed before anyone knows what the business actually sells. Wait until the pitch has settled.

Skip until it earns its place

Anything printed in a quantity you chose because it was the cheapest per unit rather than because you will use it.
