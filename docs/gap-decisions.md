# Gap decisions

How the ~110 `[GAP]` markers in `copyos/projects/KAPS/copy/` are resolved so the site ships with no brackets in it.

Rule applied throughout: **nothing is invented.** Where a fact is missing, the sentence is rewritten so it no longer needs that fact. Claims about the client's operation (turnaround, inspection procedure, minimums, guarantees) are never fabricated, because the client then has to keep them.

---

## Site-wide resolutions

| # | Recurring gap | Appears | Resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Duration on each of the six process steps, standard and rush turnaround | Home, Services, all four product pages | **Artifact-led mechanism.** Each step states what the client physically receives instead of how long it takes. The positioning document already sanctions this: artifacts and durations are equal-weight mechanism ingredients. The one real commitment we hold, response within 24 hours on business days, stays and gets more prominence. |
| 2 | What Quality Check inspects, and against what | Home, Services, 3 product pages | Written as the **record-based check**: the run is measured against the specification held on file rather than against memory. That is the real mechanism and it is already the site's differentiator. No invented inspection checklist. |
| 3 | Minimum order quantities | Services, 4 product pages | Packaging keeps the real figure, **50 to 50,000 units**. Paper products get a direct answer that is true without a number: tell us the quantity, short runs are normal here. |
| 4 | Delivery coverage | Home, Services, 3 product pages | No geographic claim made. Steps close on "delivered to the date on the quote". |
| 5 | Prices | Home, Business Essentials, Occasion, Start | Kit cards ship with contents and no price. The value line stays. "What will it cost" is answered with a **how pricing works** explainer: quantity, stock, finish, turnaround. Upgraded to real numbers the moment the client supplies them. |
| 6 | Guarantee / risk reversal | Home, Contact | No guarantee invented. Risk reversal is carried by three things that are already true: the free print check is free and yours to keep, response within 24 hours, and we will tell you when you are better off printing elsewhere. |
| 7 | The "500+ corporate clients" figure | Home, About | **Dropped.** 36 named organisations only. Thirty-six that can be checked beats five hundred that cannot. |
| 8 | "Most of them are still ordering" | About, Clients | **Cut.** Unverified. Replaced with the provenance line: named clients, 1995 to 2026. |
| 9 | All 20 witness placements | Every page | **Removed** per client instruction. Deep Proof sections are replaced by roster-derived proof: the names, the sectors, the years. |
| 10 | Three case stories | Clients | **Removed.** The Clients page runs hero, sector-grouped roster, "what they have in common", close. |
| 11 | In-house versus outsourced disciplines | Services, About, Office Stationery | Claims soften from "in-house" to "under one operation" except where the brand document supports the stronger phrasing. Flagged for the client. |
| 12 | Founder name and origin story | About | Story section keeps the 1995 New Delhi founding and the Kothari Advertising and Printing Services name, and drops the founder-specific paragraph rather than inventing one. |

## Page-level resolutions

**About, the five brands.** Following the copy's own recommendation: show KAPS and KAPS Creative Lab as live, hold House of Invitations, Gravy World Letter Press and Gravy World until launch. Naming unlaunched brands on a credibility page invites "where are they?". Reversible on client instruction.

**Occasion Essentials, the sample set.** The primary CTA depends on KAPS being able to post printed sample sets. Until confirmed, the CTA routes to contact and asks for the occasion and the date. The finishes section, which is the strongest content on the page, is unaffected.

**Start Your Small Business, the five kits.** The kits do not exist. Page is built around the free artwork check and the "what you actually need" subtraction section, both of which are already written and gap-free. The kit table drops in when the client defines contents, quantity bands and prices.

## Client answer queue

Each of these is one line. None blocks the build. Each upgrades a section that is currently written around it.

1. Standard and rush turnaround for the top three products
2. Delivery coverage: Delhi NCR, all India, export
3. Minimums for visiting cards, letterheads, brochures
4. Which disciplines are in-house and which are sourced
5. Can KAPS post physical sample sets, and at what cost
6. Any guarantee KAPS can stand behind, or none
7. Prices for the four corporate kits, or a decision not to publish
8. The five starter kits: contents, quantity bands, prices
9. Phone, WhatsApp, address, hours (Pratham supplying at build)
